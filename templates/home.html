How the Feasibility Check Works

Three clear stages from initial idea to objective evaluation report.

01 Input

Enter a topic

Submit a concise research title or problem statement (up to 300 characters). Choose calibration version V1, V2, or V3 with personal computer execution constraints.

02 Pipeline

Check and score

The 6-stage pipeline validates heuristics, tests researchability, evaluates 4 rubric criteria, and runs a programmatic 3-stage validator with single-retry correction.

03 Output

Read the report

Review dimensional scores, verdict classification, and calibrated recommendations. Copy or download the full structured report formatted in academic Markdown.

Operational Boundaries • What ScholarScope Does Not Do

ScholarScope is a feasibility decision aid, not an autonomous research agent. It evaluates whether a proposed topic is achievable under specific undergraduate constraints (4 weeks timeline, personal laptop compute, and open-access literature). It does not verify the scientific correctness or empirical truth of published papers, check plagiarism, write thesis literature reviews, synthesize code, or guarantee academic committee approval. Human advisor oversight is required before project commencement.
