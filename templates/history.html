Audit Trail & Verification Protocol

Every row in this table corresponds to an immutable record appended to the cloud Google Sheet via the Google Apps Script Webhook. The backend enforces an 11-column schema: Timestamp, Topic, Prompt Version, Scope, Resources, Sources, Outcomes, Total, Verdict, Validation Status, and Diagnostic Notes. Even rejected topics (Stage 0 prompt injections) and unresearchable inputs (Stage 1 heuristic triage) are logged to maintain an unalterable audit log.
