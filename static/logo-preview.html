Academic & Design Rationale (For Viva)

  • Zero AI Clichés: Completely avoids overused tropes (no floating brains, glowing robots, lightbulbs, sparkles, or magnifying glasses).
  • The 4-Pillar Geometric Quad-Mark: The mark consists of four balanced geometric quadrants arranged around a central calibration axis. Each quadrant directly symbolizes one of the four feasibility evaluation criteria in our rubric:
    1. Top-Left: Scope (clear boundaries)
    2. Top-Right: Resources (laptop & tooling constraints)
    3. Bottom-Left: Sources (academic literature availability)
    4. Bottom-Right: Measurable Outcomes (evaluation metrics)
  • Palette & Typography: Oxford forest green (#1F4D3F) on ink (#1B1B1A) set in classical editorial serif (Newsreader), conveying rigor and academic prestige.
  • Scalability & Dark Mode: Pure SVG using var(--logo-accent) and currentColor; seamlessly inverts for dark mode and renders crisp down to 16px.
